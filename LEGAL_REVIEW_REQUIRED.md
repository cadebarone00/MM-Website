## Tracker conventions

All items below are **OPEN — review required**. No attorney or CPA engagement, signed agreement, approved policy, business decision or completed engineering work is asserted. Named owner/reviewer and calendar due date are **unassigned** for every initial item; the table supplies proposed responsible roles and the relevant release/review trigger instead of inventing people or deadlines.

Categories distinguish **attorney review**, **CPA/tax review**, **business decision**, and **engineering dependency**. One issue can require several. Engineering can supply implementation evidence but cannot resolve a legal interpretation. A lawyer's assessment cannot substitute for building/testing required controls.

## Decision/evidence record to append when reviewed

For each item record: stable ID; named accountable owner; qualified reviewer and category; exact feature/scope revision/commit; countries and audiences; dated question and decision summary; restricted evidence reference; engineering/document conditions; status; due date; next-review date and reopening triggers. Store privileged advice, contracts, tax and personal information in restricted systems, not this repository. Never place confidential advice in public issue text.

Close an item only after the relevant professional/business decision and associated implementation evidence exist. If only one part is complete, keep the item open and document the remaining dependency. A finding of non-applicability needs an explicit scoped rationale. Update corresponding feature-registry records through the existing framework after review; this tracker does not silently mark any category compliant.
