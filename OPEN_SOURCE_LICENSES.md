## How to read conditions

These are conservative engineering review prompts grounded in local license text/metadata, not definitive legal interpretations. Consult the exact shipped license/NOTICE and qualified counsel for the actual distribution or modification. Legal-review flag PENDING means review/evidence remains open; it does not assert a violation or that every dependency needs an individual paid legal opinion.

| Key | Attribution/notice review requirement |
| --- | --- |
| A-MIT | Preserve applicable copyright and permission notice in copies/substantial portions; verify included third-party notices. |
| A-APACHE | Review license-copy, retained attribution/NOTICE and modified-file notice conditions. Inspect bundled components and patent/trademark limits. |
| A-ISC | Preserve copyright and permission notice in copies. Lucide includes a separate MIT section for Feather-derived icons; retain both applicable notices. |
| A-BSD | Review preservation of copyright, conditions and disclaimers in source/binary distribution; check the non-endorsement condition for BSD-3-Clause. |
| A-0BSD | Check exact local license and bundled components; do not invent an attribution obligation from the package name. |
| A-LGPL | Professional review of linking, modification, source/relinking and redistribution obligations; preserve notices and license materials. AND expressions require review of every component license. |
| A-MPL | Review file-level copyleft, source availability and notice obligations for covered/distributed files; distinguish build-only use from shipped artifacts. |
| A-CCBY | Review credit, license reference, change indication and attribution for redistributed data/material. |
| A-CC0 | Verify dedication/fallback text and separately review third-party rights; no ownership or trademark clearance inferred. |
| A-BLUEOAK | Local minimatch LICENSE.md requires recipients to receive license text or its link; review exact scope. |
| A-PYTHON | Inspect argparse's full historical license chain, notices and modification conditions; the identifier alone is insufficient. |
| UNKNOWN — REVIEW REQUIRED | Obtain applicable license text and rights evidence; do not infer permission. |

**C-REVIEW (commercial-use note for declared licenses):** commercial deployment/distribution is not cleared by this inventory. Review the actual license grant, modifications, bundled artifacts and notice/source obligations for the proposed use; no blanket commercial-use conclusion is recorded. Copyleft is a review trigger, not an automatic ban. Unknown entries remain UNKNOWN — REVIEW REQUIRED.
