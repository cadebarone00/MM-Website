# Trip feed review - 2026-10-19

Implemented scope: Home has Upload pictures and Make a post actions, native file selection, a text/photo composer and page-memory posts with delete. Up to six JPG/PNG/WebP files, 10 MB each; 2,000 text characters. Blob URLs are revoked on discard, delete and unmount. No network upload, photo-library account connection, server storage, shared feed, video processing, persistence or metadata stripping is implemented. Refresh or unmount clears posts. Selection does not modify device originals. Rendered text is escaped by React.

Data: explicitly selected photo bytes, filenames and entered text exist in page memory only. No new recipient, vendor, payment, subscription, advertising or sponsorship flow. Device choice does not establish consent from people pictured. Privacy, personal data, UGC/media/IP rights, retention, security, organizer responsibility, provider rules and legal review remain review_required. Jurisdiction, audience, legal entity and accountable reviewers are unassigned; no production readiness or deployment claim.

Before shared uploads: authenticate membership on writes and reads; private object storage and short-lived signed access; server-side type/size/decode validation and quotas; re-encode photos to remove location/EXIF; review video metadata separately; author delete plus moderation/reporting; define retention, account/trip deletion and backup cleanup; confirm notices, rights and provider terms. Owners unassigned; due before enabling shared uploads. A private feed cannot prevent recipients saving or screenshotting media. Storing copies with another provider does not remove The Maroon's privacy responsibilities. Keep originals on devices; compressed shared copies are a proposed architecture, not implemented controls.
