## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on features used, The Maroon may process:

- account identifiers and authentication records;
- name, email address, and profile information;
- tournament memberships, roles, invitations, and organizer relationships;
- requests to create tournaments during the beta (your name, account email, tournament or group name, year, approximate player count, optional location and an optional note), and the review decision;
- golfer/player records, team assignments, handicaps, bios, and profile photos where enabled;
- tournament configuration, dates, destinations, courses, rounds, formats, schedules, and settings;
- announcements tournament organizers post to their tournament (shown to everyone who can see the tournament, or only to its players), with the poster's name shown to tournament members;
- scores, statistics, match history, standings, results, audit events, and historical records;
- support communications and operational records;
- device/browser/network data generated by hosting, security, logs, and authentication;
- media metadata or hosted media for the founding Maroon Tournament;
- external/device media references or actions where commercial `device_external` functionality is used;
- future transaction and billing records if paid products launch.

The commercial V1 product does not require The Maroon to host customer tournament photos/videos.

## 3. How Information Is Collected

Information may come from:

- the user;
- a tournament organizer entering participant information;
- authorized scorers or tournament participants;
- authentication and application infrastructure;
- service providers;
- device/browser interactions;
- later, payment or analytics providers if those features are launched.

## 4. Purposes

Information may be used to:

- create and manage accounts;
- create and configure tournaments;
- authorize tournament access;
- review requests to create tournaments and tell you the decision;
- invite participants;
- operate scoring, standings, schedules, teams, courses, and tournament history;
- provide public tournament pages according to organizer visibility settings;
- secure, troubleshoot, audit, and improve the service;
- communicate transactional notices;
- respond to support, privacy, security, and legal requests;
- enforce policies;
- comply with legal obligations;
- later, process purchases or marketing only after those features and notices are implemented.

## 5. Public and Tournament-Visible Information

Tournament organizers control certain visibility settings.

Published tournament pages may display information such as:

- tournament name and branding;
- player names;
- teams;
- handicaps if configured as public;
- courses and schedules;
- scores, standings, results, and historical records.

Email addresses and organizer-only information should not be exposed on public tournament pages.

Private tournaments should not be publicly discoverable by unauthorized visitors.

## 6. Sharing and Service Providers

The Maroon may disclose information to vendors that provide infrastructure or services needed to operate the product.

Currently known infrastructure includes:

- Supabase for database/authentication services;
- Vercel for web application hosting;
- Cloudflare R2 for existing hosted media associated with the founding Maroon Tournament.

Additional providers may later include transactional email, monitoring, analytics, and payment processors. The final published policy must list or link to an accurate current subprocessor/vendor disclosure as appropriate.

The Maroon should not state that it sells personal data unless and until counsel has reviewed the actual meaning of “sale” under applicable laws and current data practices.

## 8. Retention

Retention periods are not yet fully approved.

Current policy principle:

- preserve data only as long as necessary for service, tournament history, security, legal, accounting, dispute, backup, or other approved purposes;
- distinguish account/profile data from tournament historical records;
- define expiration for logs, invitations, backups, and deleted-account data;
- document lawful retention exceptions.

Final durations must come from the approved Data Retention Policy.

## 9. Account Deletion and Historical Records

Users will need an approved method to request account/data deletion.

Deletion may require:

- authentication/identity verification;
- transfer of tournament ownership where applicable;
- removal or anonymization of profile/account links;
- preserving limited tournament history where lawful and necessary;
- preserving legal, security, fraud-prevention, or financial records where applicable;
- allowing backups to expire according to retention schedules.

Deleting an account should not silently destroy another organizer's historical tournament record.
