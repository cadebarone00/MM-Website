# Acceptable Use Policy — Draft

**DRAFT — NOT EFFECTIVE**

Users may not use The Maroon to:

- access accounts, tournaments, databases, or systems without authorization;
- interfere with scoring, availability, security, or other users;
- upload malware, exploit code, or malicious links;
- impersonate another person or misrepresent authority;
- harass, threaten, abuse, stalk, or target others;
- submit unlawful, defamatory, fraudulent, deceptive, or infringing content;
- upload content without required rights or permissions;
- scrape, probe, reverse engineer, overwhelm, or circumvent controls except where law expressly permits;
- collect or expose personal information unlawfully;
- create tournaments for fraudulent or deceptive purposes;
- use commercial V1 features for unlawful gambling or prohibited real-money wagering;
- manipulate scores, standings, reviews, rankings, sponsorship disclosures, or tournament records deceptively;
- violate third-party platform/provider terms through The Maroon;
- use automated systems in a manner that creates unreasonable load or abuse.

The Maroon may investigate, restrict, remove, suspend, or preserve information related to suspected abuse, subject to the Terms, Privacy Policy, and applicable law.

Final enforcement, appeals, and notice language requires attorney review.
