# Refund, Cancellation & Paid Service Policy — Draft

**DRAFT — FUTURE FEATURE — NOT EFFECTIVE**

The Maroon is not yet operating the commercial paid flow described here.

Do not publish this policy until pricing, checkout, seller identity, taxes, and product entitlements are finalized.

## Decisions Required Before Launch

- one-time tournament purchase vs subscription;
- price and included features;
- when access begins;
- cancellation window;
- refund eligibility;
- duplicate purchase handling;
- tournament cancellation/weather handling;
- service outage handling;
- chargebacks;
- taxes;
- promo codes/credits;
- renewal terms if subscriptions launch.

## Suggested Structure for Counsel Review

### One-Time Tournament Purchases

State clearly:

- price;
- edition/event covered;
- included features;
- when purchase becomes nonrefundable, if any;
- refund process;
- effect of tournament cancellation;
- effect of platform-caused material service failure.

### Subscriptions

If later introduced, disclose before purchase:

- recurring amount;
- billing interval;
- trial terms;
- renewal;
- cancellation method;
- effective cancellation date;
- price-change process;
- refund rules.

### Payment Processor

Payment-card details should be handled through the approved payment processor rather than stored directly by The Maroon wherever practical.
