# Privacy Rights Request Policy — Draft

**DRAFT — INTERNAL + FUTURE PUBLIC PROCESS**

## Purpose

Provide a consistent process for requests involving:

- access;
- correction;
- deletion;
- portability/download;
- opt-out where applicable;
- consent/preferences;
- appeal where applicable.

## Intake Channels

Planned:

- authenticated in-product request flow;
- web request form;
- `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`.

Do not require a user to expose sensitive information unnecessarily.

## Verification

Verify the requester to a degree appropriate to the request and risk.

Potential methods:

- authenticated session;
- verified email;
- additional account/tournament context;
- authorized-agent verification where applicable.

Do not request government ID by default unless approved and necessary.

## Workflow

1. Record request date/type.
2. Verify requester.
3. Determine applicable jurisdiction/right.
4. Identify systems/vendors containing responsive data.
5. Review exceptions/retention requirements.
6. Fulfill, partially fulfill, or deny with documented rationale.
7. Propagate required actions to processors/vendors.
8. Record completion/evidence.
9. Provide appeal path where required.
10. Ensure restored backups do not permanently resurrect deleted data beyond approved backup retention.

## Historical Tournament Records

Account deletion and tournament-history deletion are separate questions.

Where approved, historical scores/results may be retained in anonymized or de-linked form while account/profile information is deleted.

## Deadlines

Do not hardcode response deadlines in this draft. Counsel should map deadlines to launch jurisdictions and request type.

## Records

Privacy-request records should contain only what is necessary to prove handling and should have their own retention schedule.
