## User Representations

Users should represent that they have authority to submit and use:

- tournament logos;
- team logos;
- photographs;
- profile images;
- videos;
- music/audio;
- course/club materials;
- sponsor assets;
- written content;
- other third-party intellectual property.

Uploading content does not transfer third-party rights to The Maroon.

## Golf-Specific Third-Party Content

Course, club, event, tour, resort, team, sponsor, and equipment-company names/logos may be protected.

The Maroon should not imply affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement without authorization.

Organizers should be able to remove or replace disputed assets.

## Media

Commercial V1 supports `none` or `device_external` media. Device/external storage does not eliminate copyright, publicity, recording-consent, music, or privacy obligations.

The founding Maroon Tournament may retain existing hosted media. That exception does not authorize commercial hosted-media use.

## Removal

The Maroon may remove or restrict content believed to violate law, rights, platform rules, or service policies.

Copyright-specific complaints are addressed in the Copyright/DMCA draft.
