# Sponsorship, Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure Policy — Draft

**DRAFT — FUTURE FEATURE — NOT EFFECTIVE**

The Maroon may eventually earn revenue from sponsorships, advertisements, and affiliate relationships.

## Disclosure Principle

Paid or compensated relationships should be clearly disclosed in a manner users can notice and understand.

Potential labels include:

- Sponsored
- Advertisement
- Presented by
- Affiliate link
- Paid partnership

## Editorial Independence

Paid relationships should not secretly determine editorial rankings, reviews, course scores, or tournament results.

If compensation affects placement or presentation, disclose that relationship.

## Affiliate Links

If The Maroon may receive compensation from a user's purchase or booking, place an appropriate disclosure near the relevant recommendation/link rather than relying only on a distant legal page.

## Sponsor Assets

Sponsors must provide or authorize the logos, copy, images, and claims they want displayed.

## Restricted Categories

Before accepting ads/sponsors, create an approved category policy covering prohibited or restricted products/services, audience concerns, minors, regulated products, gambling, misleading claims, and brand-safety requirements.

## Tracking

Do not add advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, or targeted advertising without updating:

- Privacy Policy;
- Cookie Policy/preferences;
- feature compliance review;
- vendor inventory;
- jurisdiction analysis.
