# Commercial Email & Notification Policy — Internal Draft

**DRAFT — INTERNAL**

## Separate Message Types

### Transactional / Service

Examples:

- tournament invitation;
- account verification;
- password reset;
- scoring/tournament operational notice;
- security notice;
- privacy-request response.

### Marketing

Examples:

- product announcements;
- promotional offers;
- paid tournament upsells;
- newsletters intended primarily to promote The Maroon.

Do not treat marketing consent as required for ordinary account/tournament service.

## Marketing Controls

Before marketing email launches:

- maintain consent/preference source where required;
- use truthful sender/header information;
- avoid deceptive subject lines;
- include required sender/business contact information;
- provide a clear unsubscribe method;
- honor suppression/opt-out;
- ensure vendors honor suppression lists;
- document message classification.

## Tournament Invitations

Review organizer authority to provide invitee email addresses.

Invitations should:

- identify the tournament/organizer;
- avoid unrelated promotional content unless properly handled;
- provide support/reporting path;
- expire tokens appropriately;
- avoid exposing account-existence information.

## SMS / Push

Do not treat email rules as sufficient for SMS or push. Review separately before launch.
