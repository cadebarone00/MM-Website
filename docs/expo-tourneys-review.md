# Expo Tourneys feature review

Review started 2026-11-25. The first native slice reads the existing account-scoped Tourneys endpoint and displays tournament names, course, dates and past history. It adds no writes, new provider or payment flow. Later create, join, public discovery, invitations, scoring and organizer flows need their own scope assessment as implemented. Deployment and real-phone verification are outstanding; accountable owners, audience, markets, retention, vendors and professional reviewers remain unassigned.

All categories in FEATURE_COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST.md remain **review_required**. Engineering must verify bearer authentication, account switching, server authorization, visibility, failure handling and data minimization. Privacy, personal data, retention and deletion, organizer responsibilities, minors, user-generated content and public sharing need policy and professional assessment. Code, fonts, assets and golf content require intellectual-property and dependency rights review. Payments, subscriptions, advertising and sponsorship are not introduced in this slice, but applicability remains unassessed. Platform/provider rules and qualified legal review remain unresolved. This record does not authorize release.
